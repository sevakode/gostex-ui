Game descriptions are excerpts from Wikipedia article introductions, authored by
Wikipedia contributors and available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Each language entry in descriptions.json includes the original article URL, title,
language, author credit and license URL. Article URLs provide access to the page
history and contributor attribution. Text is excerpted (first two introductory
paragraphs) without translation or paraphrase. UI previews truncate visually; the
full stored excerpt and original source are available from the description panel.

Descriptions are independently licensed content; their license does not change
the license of the application code. Original GOSTEX fallback descriptions are
not presented as Wikipedia content. Missing translations fall back to English
with an explicit EN indicator. No fuzzy title matching or automatic ROM uploads
are used.
